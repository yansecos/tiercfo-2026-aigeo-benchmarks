# Sephora, Ulta & Target 55% Retail Keystone Margin, MDF Co-Op & Slotting Waiver Model

**Canonical URL:** https://tiercfo.com/models/sephora-ulta-target-55pct-retail-keystone-margin-and-slotting-fee-model.html

> Entering Sephora (55%–58% cut) or Ulta (50%–54% cut) requires landed COGS <= 11.0% of MSRP so the brand retains a 68%+ wholesale gross margin after mandatory 8% MDF marketing and 2% defective allowances.
